# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis** project explores Netflix's content library to uncover patterns in content distribution, genre popularity, maturity ratings, release years, and the balance between movies and TV shows.

Using an interactive dashboard, this project transforms raw content data into meaningful insights that help us understand Netflix's content strategy and the evolution of its entertainment catalog.

The dashboard allows users to filter content by **Year Released** and **Year Added to Netflix**, making it easier to explore trends and compare different segments of the platform's library.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of movies and TV shows on Netflix.
- Identify the most frequently occurring genre combinations.
- Understand the distribution of content across maturity ratings.
- Explore the number of seasons in TV shows.
- Examine key content metrics through interactive visualizations.
- Present findings in a clear, business-oriented dashboard.

## 🔍 Data Storytelling & Key Insights

### 1. Movies vs. TV Shows: Understanding the Content Mix

The dashboard reveals that movies account for approximately **69.6%** of the catalog, while TV shows represent around **30.4%**.

This indicates that movies form the larger share of the available content, while TV shows contribute a substantial portion of the library through episodic storytelling and multi-season series.

**Business takeaway:** Netflix's catalog contains a strong movie offering alongside a significant TV show collection, allowing it to cater to different viewing preferences.

### 2. Genre Analysis: What Types of Content Stand Out?

The top genre combinations shown in the dashboard include:

- Dramas and International Movies — 362
- Documentaries — 359
- Stand-Up Comedy — 334
- Comedies and Dramas — 274
- Dramas and Independent Movies — 252
- Kids' TV — 220

Documentaries and stand-up comedy also feature prominently among the leading categories.

**Business takeaway:** The leading categories span drama, international content, documentaries, comedy, and family entertainment. This suggests that Netflix's catalog serves a variety of audience interests rather than focusing on a single content type.

*Note: Some category labels represent combinations of genres rather than individual genres.*

### 3. Content Ratings: Understanding Audience Segments

The rating distribution chart shows a strong presence of **TV-MA and TV-14** content, with TV-PG and other ratings also represented.

This provides a view of how the catalog is distributed across different maturity classifications.

**Business takeaway:** Rating analysis can help identify the balance between mature-audience content and programming intended for broader or younger audiences. However, ratings alone do not establish the actual age demographics of Netflix viewers.

### 4. TV Show Seasons: Exploring Series Length

The dashboard includes a breakdown of TV shows by number of seasons. The visible chart indicates that **single-season shows form a particularly large group**, with a displayed count of 1,793.

This highlights the importance of examining series length instead of treating every TV show as an equivalent unit of content.

**Business takeaway:** Season-count analysis can help distinguish limited or shorter series from longer-running shows and support further investigation into content formats.

### 5. Content Duration: A Quick View of Viewing Length

The average duration KPI is approximately **69.85 minutes**.

Because the dashboard focuses on both movies and TV shows, this metric should be interpreted carefully: movie duration is usually measured in minutes, whereas TV show duration is often recorded in seasons. The metric's exact meaning depends on how duration was cleaned and calculated in the underlying dataset.

**Business takeaway:** Separating movie runtime from TV show season counts would provide a more meaningful comparison of viewing length.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- **Year Released Filter:** Explore content according to its original release year.
- **Year Added to Netflix Filter:** Analyze the content added to Netflix over different periods.
- **Genre Distribution:** Compare the most frequently occurring genre categories.
- **Movies vs. TV Shows:** Understand the catalog's content mix.
- **Rating Distribution:** Examine the spread of maturity ratings.
- **TV Show Season Analysis:** Investigate the number of seasons across TV shows.
- **KPI Summary:** Review high-level metrics in one place.

These features allow users to move from an overall view of the catalog to more focused analysis using interactive filters.

## 💡 Overall Business Insights

The analysis highlights five important observations:

1. Movies represent the majority of the catalog, while TV shows remain a substantial content segment.
2. Drama, international content, documentaries, and comedy feature prominently in the leading genre categories.
3. TV-MA and TV-14 are prominent ratings in the displayed distribution.
4. Single-season shows represent a large group in the visible season-count chart.
5. Interactive filtering makes it easier to explore how Netflix's catalog varies by release year and year added.

These observations describe the dataset rather than proving Netflix's business performance, audience preferences, or content investment strategy. Further analysis would be required to evaluate those outcomes.
